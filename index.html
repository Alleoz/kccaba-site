FAQ

Insurance Coverage FAQ

Common questions about insurance, Medicaid, Medicare, and ABA therapy coverage.

Does KD Care Community accept insurance for ABA therapy?

+

Yes. KD Care Community accepts many insurance plans for ABA therapy in Michigan, including Medicaid and Medicare. Coverage still depends on the family's specific policy and plan requirements.

Do I still need to contact my insurance company?

+

Yes. Families should confirm benefits directly with their insurance company, including network status, covered services, deductibles, copays, coinsurance, and authorization requirements.

Is ABA therapy always covered if my insurance company is listed?

+

No. A listed insurance company does not guarantee coverage. Each plan can have different eligibility rules, medical necessity standards, documentation requirements, and network arrangements.

Insurance Disclaimer

KD Care Community cannot guarantee insurance coverage or payment. Families should contact their insurance company to confirm benefits, eligibility, authorization requirements, covered services, out-of-pocket costs, and whether KD Care Community is in network for their specific plan. Coverage information can change and varies by policy.
